About MindCanvas

Our Beginning: A Decade of Depth, Millions of Students

MindCanvas began with a decade of persistence. Since 2015, the UESTC MediaLab team has reached Sichuan, Gansu, Tibet, Guizhou and more. In remote schools, we have served over 10 million K–12 students.

While promoting educational equity, we saw a critical gap: mental health is now the most hidden, urgent pain point in youth development. True educational fairness is not only about knowledge—it is about protecting every young mind.

Our Mission: Painting Clear Skies for the Mind with AI

Backed by University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in AI and embodied robotics, we founded MindCanvas.

We build a full-path digital mental health ecosystem—Perceive, Accompany, Intervene—to fill the gap in professional resources, so every campus and student has access to professional, warm, timely support.

Our Core: Hard Tech Meets Human Warmth

  • Data foundation: Validated on China's first 5M+ usage AI mental health product; models tailored to Chinese youth.
  • Multimodal understanding: Analyzes voice, facial expressions, and physiological signals to capture emotional cues beyond words.
  • Embodied interaction: Human-like robots and digital avatars reduce psychological barriers for natural, compliant, clinically sound communication.

Technology is the path to educational equity and mental freedom. MindCanvas will continue to provide the strongest safety net for every growing mind through AI.
